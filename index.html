What it is

Built to be reused across domains.

Keep the structure. Change the voice. CSS variables hold your colors and fonts; site overlays hold domain-specific copy and CTAs.

  • Brand tokens

    Colors, type, and atmosphere live in one file so a new product does not need a redesign from scratch.

  • Product strip

    Seven sibling-product slots under the brand (desktop) or a bottom 2×4 grid (phone/tablet).

  • Static deploy

    Plain HTML and CSS. Optional vanilla JS for motion and config. Drop into a cPanel document root.

How it works

Three steps to go live.

Clone the kit, override tokens and config, upload to hosting.

  1. Clone

    Copy the repository and open index.html locally.

  2. Customize

    Edit css/tokens.css and js/config.js, or load overlays from sites/.

  3. Deploy

    Upload the site files to your domain’s document root.
